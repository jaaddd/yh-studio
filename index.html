GOOD TO KNOW

A few
little details.

How do I book my visit?

Use “Plan your visit” to prepare your inquiry, then send it to Yassmeen on Instagram. Your appointment is confirmed directly by the studio; selecting a preferred day here does not reserve a time.

Where can I find prices?

Ask Yassmeen for the current menu, pricing and availability. Include your service interests and inspiration so she can guide you.

Can I ask about more than one service?

Yes. Select hair, facials, nails or any combination in the visit planner. Yassmeen can confirm what can be arranged together.

Are the inspiration photos studio results?

They are editorial reference photos to help you collect ideas, not client results. Visit Yassmeen’s Instagram for her own content.
